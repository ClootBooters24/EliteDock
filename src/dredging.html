On-the-Water Dredging for Grand Lake

Mechanical Dredging, GRDA Compliant

Elite Dock is the first docking business on Grand Lake O' the Cherokees to provide on-the-water dredging. We operate a mechanical dredging system to efficiently remove shallow sediment and larger debris, working strictly within GRDA guidelines.

Benefit: Protect Boats and Equipment

We clear underwater obstructions like large logs, rocks, and other unwieldy debris that can damage your boat's hull and props.

Benefit: Safer Slip Access

Dredging prevents grounding when maneuvering your boat in your slip and around your dock.

Benefit: Cleaner Swimming Areas

Removing decaying organic matter lessens blue-green algae bloom activity and helps create a cleaner swimming environment.
