Dock Maintenance and Repair

Keep your dock performing like it should. From routine maintenance to storm damage repairs, Elite Dock provides reliable service to extend the life of your dock, lift, and walkways.

Core Maintenance Services

Routine Service and Repairs

Whether it is wear and tear or unexpected damage, we will get your dock back to solid, safe condition.

Services include:

  • Storm and wind damage repair
  • Structural repairs and reinforcements
  • Dock inspections and maintenance

Additional Structural and Upgrade Services

Gangways and Access Systems

Built for safe, everyday use. Your gangway is more than just a walkway, it is how you access your dock every time you are at the lake. We design and build gangways for durability, stability, and the way you actually use your dock.

Included:

  • Custom widths for foot traffic or golf cart access
  • Composite, low-maintenance decking
  • Galvanized structure for long-term durability

Dock Stabilization (Stiff Arms)

Reduce movement. Increase stability. For larger docks or rough water conditions, stiff arm systems help minimize unwanted movement and create a more secure, rigid dock setup.

Benefits:

  • Reduces dock shifting and pivot
  • Improves stability in rough water
  • Multiple configurations based on dock size and location

Wave Protection Systems (Breakwaters)

Protect your dock from constant wear. Wave attenuators help break up incoming wake and reduce the stress placed on your dock, boat, and shoreline.

Benefits:

  • Minimizes wave impact and damage
  • Extends the life of your dock
  • Improves safety around your dock area

Dock Relocation

Move your dock with confidence. Whether you are upgrading, relocating, or adjusting to changing water conditions, Elite Dock can safely move and reposition your dock.

Flotation Replacement

Stay compliant and protected. We replace outdated or failing flotation with modern encapsulated systems designed for long-term performance and lake compliance.

Benefits:

  • Meets current GRDA regulations
  • Improves dock stability
  • Long-lasting flotation performance

Dock Additions and Expansions

Make your dock work better for you. From additional slips to storage upgrades, we help expand your dock to fit your needs.

Options include:

  • Additional boat slips
  • Storage or utility structures
  • Layout expansions

Decking Replacement

Upgrade the look and feel of your dock. Replace worn or outdated decking with modern materials that improve durability, safety, and appearance.

Material options:

  • Composite decking
  • Pre-cast concrete
  • Treated lumber decking

Anchoring Systems

Built for your specific conditions. Your dock's anchoring system is critical to long-term performance. We install the right system based on water depth, conditions, and dock design.

Options include:

  • Sea anchor systems
  • Telescoping systems
