Dock Add-Ons and Accessories

Upgrade your dock for better protection, easier access, and a more comfortable lake experience.

Available Add-Ons

HydroHoist Boat Lifts

Protect your investment and simplify your time on the water with a premium boat lift system. As an authorized HydroHoist dealer, Elite Dock provides industry-leading lift solutions designed for durability, ease of use, and long-term performance.

Benefits:

  • Keeps your boat out of the water (reduces wear and algae buildup)
  • Quick, effortless launching
  • Extends lifespan of your boat
  • Built for changing lake conditions

"We offer hoists for all types of pleasure boats from small personal watercraft to 40,000 lb cruisers moored in virtually any type of boat dock, boat house, or boat slip." - HydroHoist

Lift Walkways

Move around your boat with confidence. Custom lift walkways wrap around your boat lift, giving you safe and stable access for loading, cleaning, and maintenance.

Benefits:

  • Safer boarding and unloading
  • Stable footing in all conditions
  • Clean, integrated dock design

Lake Lifter

Free up deck space by adding a pole mount boat lift control unit.

Benefits:

  • Replaces manual hand wheels and outdated setups
  • Keeps sound down with quiet DC power

PWC Ports

Keep your wave runner secure, clean, and ready to ride. We offer multiple PWC port styles designed to fit your dock layout and watercraft.

Benefits:

  • Easy drive-on / drive-off access
  • Keeps PWC out of water when not in use
  • Reduces maintenance and damage

EZ-N Dock Rollers

Protect your boat and dock from costly damage. Dock rollers act as a buffer during docking, allowing your boat to glide safely into position.

Benefits:

  • Prevents scratches and dents
  • Ideal for windy or rough water conditions
  • Reduces impact stress on your dock

Elite Dock Guard

Extra protection where it matters most. Elite Dock Guard systems are designed to absorb impact and protect both your dock and your boat from everyday wear.

Benefits:

  • Cushions contact points
  • Extends dock lifespan
  • Clean, professional look

Ladders

We are happy to be an authorized Wet Steps dealer. Safe and easy access in and out of the water. Durable, marine-grade ladders that are powder-coated in a variety of colors.

Benefits:

  • Easy water entry and exit
  • Slip-resistant design
  • Built for long-term outdoor use
  • Pet friendly

Dock Bench

Comfortable, powder-coated aluminum benches built for lake life. Custom dock benches give you a dedicated space to relax, fish, or spend time with family, all while blending seamlessly into your dock's design.

Benefits:

  • Adds functional seating and usable space
  • Designed to match your dock layout
  • Durable, weather-resistant construction

Elite Custom Hammocks

Relax over the water with a custom hammock setup that is a perfect upgrade for any dock.

Benefits:

  • Over-the-water lounging experience
  • Crafted to match your dock's design and flow
  • Durable no-stretch netting

No Wake Buoy Installation

Keep your dock protected from unnecessary damage. Strategically placed no wake buoys help control boat traffic near your property, reducing wake and minimizing long-term stress on your dock and shoreline. Elite Dock handles placement and installation to ensure optimal positioning and visibility.

Benefits:

  • Minimizes wake damage and erosion
  • Protects your dock, lift, and boats
  • Promotes safer, more controlled water traffic
